# Privacy Policy: SSI-263 Speech

**Last updated:** October 10, 2026

SSI-263 Speech is an open-source speech synthesizer. It emulates the Votrax SC-02 / SSI-263 speech chip and the
talking devices built around it, and it runs entirely on your device.

## Data collection

SSI-263 Speech does **not** collect, store, transmit or share any personal data:

- no analytics or telemetry
- no network connections
- no user accounts or sign-in
- no crash reporting of its own
- no advertising or tracking

All speech is made on your device. The text it speaks, whether from VoiceOver, Spoken Content, another app or the
app's own preview, is processed on the device and never sent anywhere.

## Your files

The app carries no firmware. When you import firmware you own, the app reads only the file or files you choose. It
keeps the firmware it recognises in its own storage on your device, shared only with its own speech extension, and
removes it whenever you choose "Remove all firmware". Nothing you import leaves your device.

## Permissions

SSI-263 Speech needs no special permissions. Files are reached only through the system's file picker, one choice at
a time, and speech is provided through the operating system's standard speech-synthesizer extension.

## Third-party services

SSI-263 Speech uses no third-party services, SDKs or APIs that collect data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

## Contact
